Visual direction: Apple Health x Calm x modern physiotherapy clinic x intelligent AI motion coach.
Do NOT make it look like a generic fitness app, gym app, medical dashboard or AI chatbot.
The product should feel: calm, premium, trustworthy, clinical when necessary, warm and human, modern, minimal, motion-focused, easy for non-technical users.

1. DESIGN THEME: "CALM MOTION"
Colors:
- Deep Forest #123B35
- Soft Sage #DDEBE4
- Off White #F7F8F5
- Warm Sand #E9DFCF
- Muted Lavender #E7E3F3
- Soft Coral #E9A99A
- Primary text #17201D
- Secondary text #69736F
Avoid: neon colors, excessive gradients, cyberpunk look, dark AI dashboards, excessive glassmorphism, excessive shadows, aggressive gym aesthetics, red-heavy medical interfaces.
Use: large whitespace, soft surfaces, 20-24px card radius, 14-16px input radius, 16px button radius, elegant typography, subtle borders, gentle micro-interactions.
Typography: Manrope (or Inter). Hierarchy: 32px main headings, 24px section headings, 18px card titles, 16px body, 14px secondary text, 12px metadata.

2. CORE PRODUCT IDEA: LIVE MOTION COACH
The phone camera observes the user's movement and gives simple real-time guidance. Do not show technical AI information such as "Pose confidence: 87.4%". Show human guidance such as "Lower your right shoulder slightly." and then "Good alignment". The user should feel: "The app is watching my movement and helping me." Never overwhelm the user with technical information.

3. HOME SCREEN
Personalized. Example: "Good morning" / "How is your body feeling today?" with Great, Okay, Sore / TODAY'S SESSION: Morning Mobility, 12 min, 6 exercises, [Start Session] / YOUR PROGRESS: weekly consistency 72%, Mon Tue Wed Thu day markers. The user reaches an actual exercise within 2 taps.

4. EXPLORE SCREEN
Categories: Yoga, Physiotherapy, Mobility, Breathing, Strength, Recovery. Yoga feels peaceful and organic. Physiotherapy feels slightly more clinical and structured. The entire app must NOT look visually identical everywhere.

5. LIVE EXERCISE SCREEN (THE MOST IMPORTANT ONE)
Full-screen camera/motion interface. Structure: Back button and step counter (03 / 08) at the top, a LIVE indicator, camera view with a person + subtle skeleton overlay, exercise name (e.g. Warrior II), check items ("Spine aligned", "Knee position"), rep counter (8 / 10), a thin progress bar, and Pause and Next buttons at the bottom. Highlight the relevant joint when a correction is needed. Example corrections: "Raise your arm slightly.", "Keep your back straight.", "Move your knee inward.", "Good movement." Give ONE important correction at a time. Do not cover the person's body with UI.

6. YOGA EXPERIENCE
Calm, spacious, premium. Collections: Morning Yoga, Flexibility, Stress Relief, Mobility, Beginner, Advanced. Cards use beautiful visuals. Each pose screen has: pose name, visual demonstration, breathing guidance, alignment tips, duration, Start Motion Tracking. Optional anatomy/alignment visualization that is not visually complicated.

7. PHYSIOTHERAPY EXPERIENCE
Structured recovery experience. Example: MY RECOVERY / Shoulder Mobility / Week 3 of 6 / 78% complete / Today's exercises: Wall Slides (done), Shoulder Rotation (done), Scapular Retraction, Stretch / "Pain today?" slider 0 to 10 / [Continue]. Keep medical terms understandable. Never make unsupported medical claims.

8. PROGRESS SCREEN
Extremely simple. No dashboards full of graphs. Example: weekly consistency 78%, Sessions 5 / 6, Exercises 31, Best streak 8 days, plus "Movement improvement" for Shoulder Mobility shown as Week 1 -> Week 2 -> Week 3 with simple visuals.

9. PROFILE
Profile photo, name, goals, current programs, preferences, notifications, camera permissions, privacy, connected therapist. Clean.

10. THERAPIST MODE
A separate, more clinical professional dashboard. Example: "Good morning, Dr. Anita" / 12 patients / Arjun - Shoulder rehab - On track / Priya - Knee rehab - Missed sessions / Rahul - Back mobility - Needs review. Patient detail: recovery %, adherence %, pain trend, movement progress, recent sessions, [Adjust Program].

11. NAVIGATION
Only 4 main items: Home, Explore, Progress, You.

12. MOTION DESIGN
Subtle, purposeful animation. Ready -> 3 -> 2 -> 1 -> Start. Correct movement: "Good movement" with a check. Incorrect movement: subtle joint highlight + one correction. Exercise completion: soft expanding check animation. No confetti, no gamification.

13. UX PRINCIPLES (follow strictly)
1) One primary action per screen. 2) Minimize cognitive load. 3) Do not overwhelm users with medical information. 4) Make exercise instructions visual. 5) Make the camera experience extremely clear. 6) Large touch targets. 7) Accessibility is a priority. 8) Never rely on color alone for important feedback. 9) Provide clear loading, error and permission states. 10) Design for one-handed mobile use. 11) Keep important actions within thumb reach. 12) Never make the user search for their current exercise.

14. IMPORTANT VISUAL RULE
It must NOT look like "AI + healthcare dashboard". It must look like a beautiful wellness app with intelligent motion technology underneath. The intelligence is experienced through the interaction, not through flashy AI branding.

15. TECHNICAL UI REQUIREMENTS
Mobile-first, responsive, component-based, clean reusable components, smooth transitions, proper loading / empty / error states, camera permission state, motion tracking unavailable state, exercise completion state, offline-friendly UI. React + Tailwind CSS + Framer Motion + Lucide icons. No excessive UI libraries that make the app look generic.

16. FINAL DESIGN GOAL
Opening the app: "Wow, this feels like a premium movement/wellness product." Starting an exercise: "This app is actually helping me move." Finishing: "I understand my progress." Prioritize UI/UX quality, visual hierarchy, motion, simplicity and polish over adding dozens of features.
